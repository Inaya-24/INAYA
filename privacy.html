Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 27, 2026

Inaya respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when using our service.

Information We Access

When a user connects their TikTok account, Inaya may access information authorized by the user, including basic profile information, profile statistics, and public video information.

How We Use Information

Information is used only to provide the requested features, including displaying account information, analyzing account statistics, accessing video information, and publishing content when authorized by the user.

Data Sharing

Inaya does not sell users' personal information. Information is only processed as necessary to provide the service and its TikTok integration.

Data Security

Reasonable measures are used to protect information accessed through the service.

User Control

Users may revoke Inaya's access to their TikTok account through their TikTok account settings.

Contact

For privacy questions or requests, please contact the operator of Inaya.
